### Dataset Overview

# ISIC-2017

### Data Description

The ISIC 2017 dataset is the second-year archive of the "Skin Lesion Analysis toward Melanoma Detection" challenge, hosted at ISBI 2017 by the International Skin Imaging Collaboration (ISIC). Unlike ISIC 2016, it introduces a dedicated Validation split in addition to Training and Test, and each split follows the same internal structure (Part 1, Part 2, Part 3, images, metadata).
