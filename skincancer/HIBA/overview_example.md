### Dataset Overview

# Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires - Skin Lesions

### Data Description

This dataset is a contributor collection within the ISIC (International Skin Imaging Collaboration) archive, sourced from Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires. It contains dermoscopic and clinical images of skin lesions with associated diagnostic labels, patient demographics, and anatomical metadata, used primarily for skin lesion classification and melanoma detection research.

- **What the dataset contains**: Dermoscopic and clinical (close-up/overview) images of skin lesions with diagnosis labels, benign/malignant status, anatomical site, Fitzpatrick skin type, and patient/lesion identifiers.
- **Business purpose**: Support development and benchmarking of automated skin lesion classification algorithms, including across-institution generalization studies.
- **Primary use cases**: Melanoma/skin cancer classification, diagnosis-hierarchy modeling, skin type / demographic bias analysis in dermatology AI.
- **Key entities or metrics included**: Lesion images (dermoscopic + clinical), diagnosis hierarchy (3 levels), benign/malignant labels, Fitzpatrick skin type, patient and lesion identifiers.
