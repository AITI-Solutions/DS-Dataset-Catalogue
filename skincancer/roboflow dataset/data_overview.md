### Dataset Overview

# Waterlo_image_masks

The dataset consists of skin lesion images and their corresponding mask images. Each sample includes an original JPEG (.jpg) image and a corresponding PNG (_mask.png) image indicating the segmented lesion region. In total, the dataset contains 580 image files, consisting of 290 original images and 290 corresponding mask images.
